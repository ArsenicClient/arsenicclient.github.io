Release changelog

What changed in each Arsenic release. Full notes and downloads are on GitHub.

v3.0

Oct 10, 2026
  • No longer a Forge mod: an external injector that attaches to vanilla, Lunar or Forge at runtime.
  • Addon/pack system with Bedwars and Pit packs, hot-loaded (similar to scripting systems in other clients).
  • Lots more modules.

v2.0

Oct 5, 2026
  • Simplified settings for a lot of modules.
  • Added addons/scripts.
  • Lots of other changes.

v1.1

Jul 19, 2026
  • Major UI updates.
  • New modules: AutoBlockIn, GlowESP, Chams.
  • Various other changes.
