HitflickBlatantTurns away for one tick as you hit, to knock the target in the direction you pick.

Your server-side aim flicks to the chosen side for one tick, then the hit lands, so the knockback goes that way. Void simulates the knockback and only flicks when it would push the target off an edge; it needs extra knockback (sprinting or a Knockback enchant) and draws an arrow showing the push. Void2 picks the side of the target with more open drop. No hit goes out if the server sees you blocking.
