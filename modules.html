Highlights

  • KillAura — the blatant-tier combat module, with configurable ring and 3D visual styles and a heuristics aim mode that favours human-looking rotations.
  • AimAssist and Backtrack — lower-profile combat helpers.
  • Scaffold, TellyScaffold, BridgeAssist and Clutch — bridging and block-placement movement modules.
  • Stasis — hangs in the air for up to 45 ticks; flag-prone and opt-in.

Module list reflects the v3.0 source tree and changes between releases; see the changelog. Need something not here? Check the addon packs for Bedwars, Pit, HUD and render modules, or write your own.
