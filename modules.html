HitflickBlatantTurns away for one tick as you hit, to knock the target in the direction you pick.

Your server-side aim flicks to the chosen side for one tick, then the hit lands, so the knockback goes that way. Void simulates the knockback and only flicks when it would push the target off an edge; it needs extra knockback (sprinting or a Knockback enchant) and draws an arrow showing the push. Void2 picks the side of the target with more open drop. No hit goes out if the server sees you blocking.

Player 12 modules

AutoBlockInLegitWalls you in with blocks: four sides and a roof.

Once you stand on the ground it locks your spot and plans every block: one over your head, then both blocks on each side, starting with the side you face. When a spot can't be reached it places support blocks first. The strongest blocks in your hotbar go into the walls (sponge, obsidian, end stone, glass, wood, clay, then wool) and the weakest into supports. It aims with silent rotations and draws the planned blocks in your theme colors.

Turns itself off when it's done, or if you leave your block, have nothing under you or don't have enough blocks in your hotbar. Switching slot yourself pauses it until you go back, and your slot comes back at the end.

Custom main menuBuilt-inArsenic's own main menu.

Not a module, so it's always on. Its Vanilla Menu button shows the vanilla menu once; the next time you get Arsenic's menu again.

Nine themes: Classic (default), Ocean, Void, Specter, Ember, Jade, Obsidian, Sakura and Toxin. Each sets the colors the ClickGUI, HUD and visuals use, and its own shader background in the ClickGUI. Your choice is saved with the client config.
